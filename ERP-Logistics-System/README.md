During my time at Cartzlink, I worked on ERP and order-fulfillment systems for SME retail clients — covering inventory, point-of-sale, invoicing, and sales, plus real-time integration with multiple courier networks for shipment tracking.

## 🎯 Business Problem

SME retail clients needed reliable systems to manage inventory across sales channels, process a real daily volume of orders without manual bottlenecks, and give customers accurate shipment tracking across multiple courier partners — all without the discrepancies and manual reconciliation that come from disconnected tools.

## 💡 Solution

I built and maintained 5+ ERP modules across Laravel, Yii, and CodeIgniter, integrated 4+ third-party courier APIs for real-time tracking, and reworked the point-of-sale inventory-sync logic to keep stock counts consistent across channels.

## 👤 My Role

PHP Developer contributing across multiple ERP modules and client systems — not sole-owner of a single application, but responsible for meaningful, production-facing modules within each.

## 🧰 Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
| --- | --- |
| **Backend frameworks** | Laravel, Yii, CodeIgniter |
| **Integrations** | 4+ third-party courier/shipment-tracking APIs |
| **Architecture** | RESTful API design |

## 🧩 Major Modules

- **Inventory** — stock tracking and synchronization across sales channels.
- **Point of Sale (POS)** — in-store/order processing with inventory sync.
- **Invoicing & Sales** — order and billing workflows.
- **Courier / Shipment Tracking** — real-time tracking across multiple courier integrations.

## 🔍 Technical Highlights

- Worked across three different PHP frameworks (Laravel, Yii, CodeIgniter) within the same role, adapting to each codebase's existing conventions rather than standardizing on one.
- Integrated 4+ distinct courier APIs into a consistent shipment-tracking experience for end customers.
- Diagnosed and resolved the root cause of stock-count drift between POS and inventory systems.

## 📈 Business Impact

- Reduced stock discrepancies by roughly **50%** through reworked POS inventory-sync logic.
- Supported **500+ daily orders** for SME clients with zero regression incidents introduced.
- Gave customers real-time shipment visibility across 4+ courier partners instead of a single-carrier view.
